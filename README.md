# Trust in Design: references and resources

Links and tools behind my talk **Designing Confidence in High-Stakes Systems**, Trust in Design, EnterpriseUX, Arnhem, 2026.

## Visual helpers

*Open-source building blocks that show what an agent is doing.*

**[Thinking Orbs](https://github.com/yogesharc/thinking-orbs)**  
yogesharc, MIT · React library  
Animated status orbs for every agent state: thinking, searching, compacting and more. Visibility you can feel. Demo at thinkingorbs.com.

**[AI Elements](https://github.com/vercel/ai-elements)**  
Vercel, Apache-2.0 · Component library  
Components for agent interfaces built on shadcn/ui: confirmation, checkpoint, plan, reasoning, chain of thought. Control and oversight, ready to use.

**[prompt-kit](https://github.com/ibelick/prompt-kit)**  
ibelick, MIT · Component library  
Clean, accessible building blocks for AI interfaces: reasoning, steps, a thinking bar and a feedback bar.

**[assistant-ui](https://github.com/assistant-ui/assistant-ui)**  
assistant-ui, MIT · React toolkit  
An open-source toolkit for AI chat, where tool calls show up as their own interface, not buried in text.

## How trust is built, lost and repaired

*The thinking behind the first half of the talk.*

**[Removing the Shadow of Suspicion: The Effects of Apology Versus Denial](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.89.1.104)**  
Kim, Ferrin, Cooper and Dirks, 2004 · Paper  
What repairs trust after a failure. For competence mistakes, an apology works better than denial. The evidence behind repairing trust.

**[Who Can You Trust?](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/rachel-botsman/who-can-you-trust/9781541773684/)**  
Rachel Botsman, 2017 · Book  
How trust is built, managed, lost and repaired in the digital age, right up to trusting platforms and bots.

## Designing agents people can trust

*Visibility, control and oversight, in practice.*

**[Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/ai-guidelines/)**  
Amershi et al., Microsoft Research, CHI 2019 · Guidelines  
Eighteen evidence-based guidelines for AI experiences, from setting expectations to handling errors. Easy to turn into a review checklist.

**[People + AI Guidebook: Explainability + Trust](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/explainability-trust/)**  
Google PAIR · Guidebook chapter  
Practical help with calibrating trust: what to explain, how to show confidence, and how trust shifts before, during and after use.

**[Our framework for developing safe and trustworthy agents](https://www.anthropic.com/news/our-framework-for-developing-safe-and-trustworthy-agents)**  
Anthropic, 2025 · Article  
Five principles for agents: human control, transparency, alignment with human values, privacy and secure interactions.

**[Measuring AI agent autonomy in practice](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy)**  
Anthropic, 2026 · Research  
Real agent use at scale. Experienced users auto-approve more, and interrupt more. Autonomy grows with trust.

**[Shape of AI](https://www.shapeof.ai/)**  
Emily Campbell · Pattern library  
A catalogue of AI interface patterns. The governor and trust builder groups cover oversight, consent and disclosure.

**[Legibility by design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2zmW-L1jtjc)**  
Ryan Powell (Waymo), Config 2026 · Talk, 21 min  
Trust in autonomous systems as a communication problem. Calibrated feedback, and showing intent, not just status.
